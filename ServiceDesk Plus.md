# What Is IT Service Management?

IT Service Management (ITSM) refers to the processes, tools, and practices used by organizations to design, deliver, manage, and improve IT services.

Using **ManageEngine ServiceDesk Plus**, organizations can efficiently manage IT service requests, track incidents, control assets, automate workflows, and ensure faster issue resolution.

By implementing ITSM, businesses can reduce downtime, improve service quality, and maintain structured IT operations aligned with business objectives.
