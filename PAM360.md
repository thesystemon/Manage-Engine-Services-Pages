# What Is Privileged Access Management?

Privileged Access Management (PAM) is a cybersecurity approach designed to secure, control, and monitor privileged accounts that have elevated permissions in an IT environment.

Using **ManageEngine PAM360**, organizations can securely store sensitive credentials, enforce access policies, track privileged sessions, and prevent unauthorized administrative access.

By implementing PAM, businesses significantly reduce the risk of credential theft, insider threats, and privilege misuse—one of the most common causes of security breaches.
